# Upwave

> Upwave (formerly Survata) is the Brand Outcomes Measurement Platform — it brings science to top-of-funnel advertising by measuring Brand Lift, validating Brand Reach, and surfacing optimization insights for brand campaigns across CTV, digital, linear TV, audio, and social.

Upwave does not publish a public developer portal or API documentation. The platform is delivered as a SaaS application at app.upwave.com, with ad-server and DSP integrations managed through the Upwave team and partner ecosystem. Upwave is a portfolio company of Y Combinator, Uncork Capital, and Initialized Capital (it began as Survata, a YC S12 survey-research company).

## Platform

- [Platform overview](https://www.upwave.com/platform/): The Brand Outcomes Measurement Platform.
- [Brand Lift](https://www.upwave.com/brand-lift/): Measure the incremental impact of advertising on brand metrics.
- [Brand Reach](https://www.upwave.com/brand-reach/): Validate who brand campaigns actually reached.
- [Brand Optimization](https://www.upwave.com/brand-optimization/): Surface what is working across campaigns.
- [Bayes](https://www.upwave.com/bayes/): Upwave's AI layer for brand measurement.
- [Partner Ecosystem](https://www.upwave.com/partner-ecosystem/): Ad platforms and DSPs Upwave integrates with.
- [Digital Network](https://www.upwave.com/digital-network/): Upwave's survey publisher network.
